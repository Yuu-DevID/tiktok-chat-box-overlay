Good to know

  • Music is the audio source. Do not tick “Shutdown source when not visible” in OBS, or songs stop when you switch scenes.
  • Chat window is a normal desktop window. Pin it with your desktop: right-click the title bar → More Actions → Keep Above Others.
  • Session totals reset when the app restarts. Filler songs are the only queue state saved to disk.
  • Requests are silent in TikTok chat. Refusals appear in the Panel, since this app only reads chat.
